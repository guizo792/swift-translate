Translation, right where you're reading.

Select a word or sentence on any web page and click the small translate icon (or right-click and choose Translate Selection). A popup opens right beside the text with the translation. Prefer no icon? Turn it off in the toolbar popup and translate from the right-click menu.

WHAT YOU GET
• Meanings, examples and similar words for single words, one click away under "See dictionary"
• Whole sentences and paragraphs, in 80+ languages, with automatic language detection
• A searchable language picker that remembers your recent languages
• Edit the original text right in the popup, and swap languages in one click
• Pronunciation for language learners: hear every translation in Google's natural voice, including languages like Arabic that many computers have no voice for
• Romanization for non-Latin scripts, so you can read how it sounds
• Saved words: star a word to keep it with its translation and the sentence it came from. Open your list from the toolbar icon, then search it or export it to CSV
• Light and dark themes
• If Google is unavailable, Chrome's built-in on-device translation is used where your browser supports it

PRIVATE
The text you select is sent to Google Translate only to translate it and read it aloud. Saved words stay on your device; your language and theme settings sync through your browser. No account, no tracking, no analytics.
